# THE HAT TOSS: original script v1 (R5 production-shot proof, 2026-11-23)
Format: one ~7 s vertical shot, one hero, one prop, one street. Original story; the Rick Western film is only the measuring stick (reference_board.json), none of its story, characters or shots is used.
Understandable with the sound off: a calm man on an empty street throws a coin straight up and does not look; it falls into his top hat.

Noon. One dusty street. One very calm gambler.
He flips a single coin straight up, and he doesn't even look.
Gravity does the rest.
Right into the hat. Every single time.

Setup: the street, the man, the calm.  Action: the flip.  Payoff: the coin lands in the hat.  Ending: close on the hat, the coin sitting in it, the man still not looking.
Cast: the gambler (hero, top hat). Place: one noon Western street. Prop: one coin (flight); one barrel beside him (scale and depth).
