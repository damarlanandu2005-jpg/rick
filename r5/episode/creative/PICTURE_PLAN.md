## Beat detail (what a different implementation may not reinterpret)
- Beat 1 (0.00-1.83 s): camera 0.55 m high, 3.6 m from him, 24 mm, he is 30 % of the frame height and horizontally centred; the barrel at his right elbow (frame left); the street runs up the frame to the vanishing point; sky fills the top third. Emphasis: the lone figure against the empty street.
- Beat 2 (1.83-4.64 s): camera moves to chest height 1.25 m, 2.0 m from him, he fills the lower two thirds, hat brim inside the frame top. Action: the coin flick on "flips". Emphasis: hat and face.
- Beat 3 (4.64-7.75 s): from "gravity" the camera (3.4 m back, 0.8 m high, aim 1.8 m) rides the coin as it falls from high in the blue sky, then settles at 2.4 m, 1.2 m high, aim 1.3 m: the whole gambler fills about 45 % of the frame height, hat at the upper third. On "hat" the coin lands on the crown and stays. Ending image: the whole gambler, hat with the coin on it, the calm stance of frame 0.
- Why the payoff is wide and not a close-up (creative, from the Ricks camera gates): the camera gates (face frames with the whole body >= 65 %, close <= 7 %) rule out a long close; reference b3_b (close) is the fallback the gates exclude, reference b3_a (an object high in the sky over the street) is the composition we match.
- Payoff: the coin is ON the hat by the word "hat" and stays there to the last frame. If the coin is not visibly on the hat at the last frame the shot fails.
