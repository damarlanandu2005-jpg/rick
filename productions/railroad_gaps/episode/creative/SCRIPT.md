# The Gap — script (production 1, 10 s)
Source: "Why Perfect Railroads Don't Work" — used as a STORY reference only (the idea: rail joints are left open on purpose because steel expands in heat; gapless track buckles). No wording, visuals, characters, camera or edit copied.

Why do railroads have gaps? Steel grows in summer heat. Track laid perfectly end to end has nowhere to go, so it pushes until it snaps sideways. The gap gives it room.

32 words, target ~3.4 words/s over 10 s.
Beats: HOOK (the gap looks like a mistake) -> PROBLEM (heat grows steel) -> EXPLANATION (gapless track has nowhere to go, buckles) -> PAYOFF (the gap is the room).
