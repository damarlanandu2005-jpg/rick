# LOOK BIBLE — "The Gap" (production 1). Our own design; the source video is a story reference only and is NOT a style reference.

## Idea in one picture
High-summer noon on an open meadow: two railway lines side by side under a hard white sun. Ours has a dark open joint every rail length; the other is one unbroken bright ribbon of steel — until it bends like a dropped rope.

## Character
- Mara, track keeper (new cast `trackkeeper`, built only from the locked kit): lady body, standard size, orange hi-vis road-worker vest with silver bands and navy trousers, brown ponytail, wide straw sun hat with a rose band (raised so her eyes always read). White meme face; expressions: standard (curious), sus (doubt), cool, proud (payoff).
- She is the warmest, most saturated thing in every frame (orange vs. green-gold meadow and blue sky). Never placed against orange.

## Environment
- One place: `heat_line` — a flat summer meadow, golden-green grass, two ballast beds of warm grey stone, timber sleepers, steel rails. Telegraph poles on the far side (rhythm and scale), soft oak trees and low hazy hills at the horizon, clean clear-day sky with light cloud.
- Left line (ours): rails in 12 m lengths, every joint a visible dark gap with fishplates and bolts (the hero joint is a Blender asset).
- Right line (the "perfect" one): one continuous rail, brighter steel, no joints; 20 m down it the rails have buckled into a sideways S (Blender asset) with sleepers dragged out of line.

## Materials
- Steel rails: mid grey with a bright running surface (the sun glints off it); fishplates/bolts darker steel; sleepers warm weathered wood; ballast warm grey; grass relief golden-green.

## Lighting & atmosphere
- Noon `day` light from high and slightly left; short, hard shadows. On "heat" a warm wash passes over the frame and the rail glints; a bead of sweat on Mara. On "snaps" a camera kick and a dust burst at the kink.

## Background treatment & hierarchy
1. Mara's face / the gap (hook, payoff) 2. the kink (explanation) 3. the two lines as leading lines 4. sky, poles, trees.
- Background stays low-contrast and soft; the joint gap is the darkest small shape near Mara; the kink is framed against plain grass, never against poles.

## Colour relationships
- Orange (Mara) vs. green-gold (meadow) vs. sky blue; steel neutral grey; the only dark accents are the gaps, the sleepers' shadows and the ink lines.

## Readability rules
- The gap must read at phone size in the hook and payoff (>= 20 px wide at 720 px). The kink must read as an S in one glance (camera nearly overhead-and-behind, along the line).

## V1.1 changes (after independent judge v1, 2026-11-24)
- Gap: cartoon scale 19 cm (prop_rail_joint_gapped@1.1), polished end faces frame it; phone-size readability over engineering realism. Our line's other joints match (19 cm).
- Contrast: ours = older darker steel (#767c83) with joints; the perfect line = bright new steel (#c3c9cf), one unbroken ribbon.
- Heat: a white-hot sun over the far end of the line, faint shimmer rising off both lines from "Steel" on, the existing warm wash on "heat". Original treatment, no borrowed effect.
- Mechanism: the perfect line shivers and bows from "pushes", throws into the S on "snaps" (8 frames, small overshoot), sleepers dragged and twisted, ballast shoved out (prop_track_buckled@1.1).
- Room: in the last shot the rail end grows into our open joint with a hot tip and stops short; the gap that is left is the answer.
- Track build: tie plates under every rail seat, sleepers bedded in crib ballast, darker timber (#4a3626).
